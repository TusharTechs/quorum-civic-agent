# QUORUM

> Your city publishes 400 pages a week. QUORUM reads all of it, and tells you
> the one paragraph that lands on your street.

**Your city has a memory. Now you do too.**

Built with the [Strands Agents SDK](https://strandsagents.com) and Amazon Bedrock
AgentCore for the Agents for Humans Hackathon.
